# Aim

To determine the impact resistance and clearance retention of protective toe caps in safety footwear under a standardized dynamic impact energy of 200 Joules (in accordance with EN ISO 20345 / IS 15298 Part 2) and 101.7 Joules (in accordance with ASTM F2413), evaluating structural integrity and compliance with minimum safety clearance requirements.

---

## Objectives
1. To understand the operational mechanics of the drop tower impact test apparatus for safety footwear.
2. To compute the required drop height and strike velocity using the principle of conservation of energy ($E = m \cdot g \cdot h$).
3. To evaluate the mechanical behavior of various toe cap materials (Steel, Aluminium, Fiberglass Composite, and Carbon Fiber) under severe dynamic impact loading.
4. To measure the minimum internal clearance retained during peak dynamic deflection using an internal non-rebounding modelling clay (plasticine) sensor.
5. To assess the specimen for structural failure modes, such as brittle fracture, through-cracking, or unacceptable plastic collapse.
